01About

Art with purpose. Play with feeling.

I'm a game artist and designer focused on the places where visual craft and player experience meet.

For the past eight years, I've helped teams turn early ideas into believable worlds. My approach combines a designer's curiosity with an artist's eye — always asking what the player should feel, then building every detail toward that answer.

I care about strong silhouettes, meaningful spaces, imperfect surfaces, and the small environmental stories that reward a player for looking closer.
